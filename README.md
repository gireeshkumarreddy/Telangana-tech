# TGTS — A connected Telangana

A buildless, responsive design concept inspired by the editorial scale and spatial discipline of the supplied Soltera reference. The complete site is in `dist/`.

Open `dist/index.html` or deploy `dist/` to any static host. There are no build dependencies or API keys.

## Features

- Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet and mobile
- Sticky navigation and compact mobile menu
- Government identity masthead with Chief Minister, IT minister and TGTS chairman portraits
- Six client logos scrolling continuously right to left directly after the hero, with pause and reduced-motion controls
- Three photographic stories covering technology, cybersecurity audits and IT careers
- Expandable service directory with a distinct photograph for every service
- Local search with service deep links
- Keyboard-accessible resource tabs and modal dialogs
- Higher contrast, text size and reduced motion controls
- Contact form that prepares an email draft; no backend submission or stored enquiries
- Google Maps embedded directly in the footer, with location details and directions
- Locally hosted photos and font

## Content

This is a clearly labelled independent design concept, not an official TGTS portal. The live TGTS website returned 502 / timed out during research on 6 October 2026. Factual content and leadership roles were checked against official Telangana sources (see `CREDITS.md`). The six clients follow the supplied brief, with separately sourced institution logos. Their exact original artwork and the complete original client roster cannot yet be compared with the inaccessible homepage. No metrics, current tender dates or vacancies have been fabricated. The supplied general email was not corroborated; the contact section uses the office phone and Managing Director email published by the Telangana IT, E&C department directory.

The revised page includes the requested leadership header, Telangana identity artwork, major-client marquee, welcome/technology imagery, cybersecurity audits, IT careers, seven service areas, events link, government orders, RTI and contact information. Complete original-content preservation remains pending a saved original page or full-page screenshots.

Named infrastructure programmes are contextual examples from official publications, not claims about current delivery, ownership or performance. Generic server, office and architecture photos are illustrative. The Secretariat is correctly identified; it is not represented as the TGTS office.

## Validation

Source checks cover JavaScript syntax, internal links, assets, unique IDs, headings, form labels and image alternatives. Browser visual testing was unavailable in the current Sites runtime because the required control-browser capability was not exposed. Responsive and accessibility styling is implemented but no formal WCAG conformance certification is claimed.

## Deployment

The Sites project identity is recorded in `.openai/hosting.json`. Preserve that identity when updating this project.
